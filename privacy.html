Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Blender Shorts Publisher ("the app") is a personal tool used by its author to post their own videos to their own accounts.

What the app receives

When the account owner approves the app, the platform gives it an access token and the account's public profile name. The app uses these only to upload and publish the owner's videos.

Where it is stored

Tokens and names are stored only on the author's own computer. The app has no server, no database, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone other than the platform being posted to.

Other people's data

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person.

Removing access

The account owner can revoke the app at any time in the connected account's settings on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook, which deletes the stored token's validity immediately.
